Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

Easyworks AI Solutions ("Easyworks", "we", "us") provides managed marketing and AI services to local businesses ("Clients"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we handle, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you. It covers our website (easyworks.ai), our software, and the marketing services we operate on behalf of our Clients — including the management and publishing of content to social media accounts (such as Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts) that a Client has explicitly authorized us to manage.

Our core commitment: We only access accounts and data that a Client has explicitly granted us permission to manage. We use that access solely to deliver the services the Client has hired us for. We never sell personal information.

1. Who this applies to

  • Visitors to easyworks.ai.
  • Clients who engage Easyworks for marketing/AI services.
  • End users of our Clients' pages whose information may be processed while we operate those pages on the Client's behalf.

2. Information we handle

Information you provide

  • Contact details submitted through forms (name, email, phone, business details).
  • Information Clients share with us to deliver services.

Information accessed on a Client's behalf (with the Client's authorization)

  • Facebook Pages & Instagram accounts: When a Client grants us access through Meta Business Manager, we use Meta's APIs to schedule and publish the marketing content we produce for that Client, and to read basic page/post performance. We only post to accounts the Client has assigned to us, and only the content we have created or that the Client has approved.
  • Page insights / metrics used to report performance and improve content.
  • Messages or comments only where a Client has asked us to assist with engagement.

Information collected automatically

  • Standard log and device data when you visit our website (e.g., IP address, browser type, pages viewed) for security and analytics.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, operate, and improve our services for Clients.
  • To create, schedule, and publish marketing content to authorized Client accounts.
  • To measure and report on content/campaign performance.
  • To communicate with Clients and respond to inquiries.
  • To maintain security and comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only:

  • With service providers / platforms that enable our services — for example, Meta Platforms (Facebook/Instagram), hosting and content tooling — strictly to perform the services requested.
  • With the Client whose accounts and data we manage.
  • When required by law, or to protect rights, safety, and security.

5. Data retention & deletion

We retain information only as long as needed to provide services or as required by law. Clients may request export or deletion of their data at any time, and any party may request deletion of their personal information.

Data deletion requests: Email team@easyworksai.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and we will action it within 30 days. To revoke our access to your Facebook/Instagram accounts at any time, remove Easyworks from your Meta Business settings.

6. Your choices & rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal information (contact us).
  • Revoke account access we hold on your behalf at any time via Meta Business settings.
  • Depending on your location, you may have rights under PIPEDA, Alberta PIPA, GDPR, or similar laws.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information, including least-privilege access that Clients can revoke at any time.

8. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
