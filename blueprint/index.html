What we read

Six vitals. Your real numbers.

01

Connection

Are your phone, website, calendar, CRM and reviews one system, or separate tools that never talk? This is the one the free Scan cannot see from outside.

02

Reflexes

How fast a new lead gets a real reply, and how many calls end in voicemail. Measured from your own call and message logs.

03

Voice

How easily customers find you: your site, your Google profile, your listings and where you rank for what they actually search.

04

Circulation

Repeat business, reviews and referrals. Whether past customers come back, and whether happy ones are ever asked to say so.

05

Immunity

How ready you are for where your market is heading over the next few years. The other vital the Scan cannot read.

06

Autonomy

How much of the business runs without the owner. We count the hours you spend on admin, follow up and scheduling.
