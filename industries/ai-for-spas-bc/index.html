The treatment

A spa at full strength.

01

Receptionist (24/7 booking)

Client calls at any hour, the call is answered and the right service booked into the right room with the right esthetician. Handles the basics ("how long does it take," "what should I wear," "is parking free") without burning your front desk's time during the day.

02

Treatment-plan follow-up

A client who comes in for a facial often needs the next one in 4-6 weeks. The reminder goes out on its own, suggests rebooking, and offers a small loyalty incentive at the right moment. Significant rebooking lift in real spas we run this for.

03

Review velocity engine

Every happy client gets a polite Google review request 24 hours after their visit. Spas where review requests run on their own hit 4.9 stars and dominate the Map Pack. Spas sitting at 4.2 stars get skipped over.

04

New-client acquisition via local SEO

Google Business Profile optimization with proper categories ("med spa" vs "day spa" vs "skincare clinic" matters more than people think), city-specific content for your service area, and review velocity working together.

05

Upsell during booking

When a client books a facial, the receptionist offers an add-on (LED therapy, lip treatment) at the right price point. Conversion is meaningful when it sounds like a friendly upgrade instead of a sales pitch.
