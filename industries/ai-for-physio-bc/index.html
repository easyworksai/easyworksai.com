The treatment

A physio clinic at full strength.

01

Receptionist with ICBC/WSBC intake

Every incoming call is answered, the right intake information captured up front (claim number, GP referral status, MVA date), and the appointment booked. Reduces front-desk load by an average of 15-20 hours a week in a 3-physio clinic.

02

Voice → Report (CL-19 + Form 8)

Our flagship for BC physio: dictate a session, get back a fully formatted ICBC CL-19 or WSBC Form 8 with the codes and language ICBC actually accepts. Physiotherapists report saving 4-7 hours per week in reporting alone.

03

Reminders + cancellations, handled

Same-day cancellations cost a clinic $80-150 a slot. SMS reminder sequences are built to cut that cancellation rate.

04

New-patient acquisition via local SEO

Google Business Profile optimization for the physio categories, ICBC injury-specific local pages, and content that ranks for "physio near me" in your specific city. Most BC physio clinic GBPs are 40-60% complete; we get to 100%.

05

Insurance billing follow-up

When a claim is short-paid or denied, it gets flagged and the appeal queued on its own. Sounds boring, recovers thousands a month.
