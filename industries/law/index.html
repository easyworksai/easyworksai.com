What we install

The law firms stack.

01

Intake receptionist, 24/7

Calls answered in the firm’s tone, screened by practice area, qualified with your intake questions and booked for a consult. Urgent matters escalate to a person by your rules. The system never gives legal advice.

02

Consult booking and reminders

Self booking, confirmations and reminders that cut consult no shows. Intake forms feed your practice management system so nothing is typed twice. Works with Clio and PracticePanther.

03

Reputation and practice area pages

Review requests at the right moment in the matter, plus practice area pages and local SEO so the firm shows up for the searches that produce signed clients.

04

High intent search ads

Managed search campaigns on the terms people use when they need a lawyer this week, with a report showing cost per signed client instead of cost per click.

05

Dictation to finished documents

Voice to Report turns dictation into formatted memos, letters and file notes so the work product is done when the call ends.
