From "hi" to live, in a week.

No 90-day implementation phase. No mystery. Here's the actual schedule:

  1. Day 0

    You book

    Pick a 15-min slot. We send a 4-question form so we walk in already knowing your business.

  2. Day 1

    The call

    15 minutes, no pitch theatre. We diagnose what's actually wrong and what it's costing you. You talk, we listen.

  3. Day 2

    Written plan in your inbox

    A real PDF: the symptoms we found, what we'd build, the exact price and the expected return. Yours to keep either way.

  4. Day 3

    You decide

    Hire us, sit on it, or show the plan to someone else. Zero follow-up pressure from us.

  5. Day 4–6

    We build on-site

    Brad drives to your shop. CRM wired, receptionist trained, integrations tested at your counter.

  6. Day 7

    Live + your team trained

    In-person walkthrough with whoever runs your front desk. We don't leave until they can run it solo.
